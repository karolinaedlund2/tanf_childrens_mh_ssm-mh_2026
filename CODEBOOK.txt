2. SURVEY DESIGN VARIABLES
================================================================================

stratum
  Description:  Original NSCH sampling stratum
  Values:       "1", "2", "2A"
  Source:       NSCH

strata
  Description:  Redefined sampling stratum for multi-year analyses, per NSCH
                guidance
  Values:       "1" = stratum 1; "2" = strata 2 and 2A combined
  Derived from: stratum

stratacross
  Description:  State x stratum interaction used as the strata variable in the
                complex survey design
  Values:       102 levels (51 states x 2 strata)
  Derived from: fipsst, strata
  Used in:      Supp. Table 4 only

fwc
  Description:  NSCH selected-child weight (topical survey)
  Type:         Continuous
  Source:       NSCH

weights
  Description:  Analysis weight: fwc rescaled to mean = 1 (equivalent up to a
                constant to fwc divided by 7 combined survey years, per NSCH
                multi-year guidance)
  Type:         Continuous
  Derived from: fwc
  Used in:      All weighted models and descriptive statistics

3. EXPOSURE
================================================================================

maxben_3fam
  Description:  State maximum monthly TANF cash benefit for a family of three
  Type:         Continuous
  Units:        USD 100 per month in TANF_master (divided by 100 so that
                coefficients correspond to a $100 increase); maxben_merged
                stores USD per month
  Timing:       Lagged 1 year in TANF_master (e.g., 2015 benefits linked to
                2016 survey year); concurrent in TANF_master_concurrent;
                lagged 2 years in TANF_master_2yrlag
  Source:       Urban Institute Welfare Rules Database
  Notes:        Rows for CA, MA, and WI collapsed across Nonexempt vs. Exempt
                categories based on guidance from the Urban Institute

4. OUTCOMES
================================================================================

All outcomes are binary (0/1), parent/caregiver reported, and based on
whether a doctor or other health care provider ever told the caregiver the
child has the condition (item "a") and whether the child currently has it
(item "b"). Models are linear probability models; coefficients are reported
in percentage points per 1 unit ($100) increase in maxben_3fam.

anxiety
  Description:  Current diagnosed anxiety
  NSCH items:   k2q33a (ever told), k2q33b (currently has)
  Values:       1 = currently has (k2q33b = 1); 0 = does not currently have
                or never told

depression
  Description:  Current diagnosed depression
  NSCH items:   k2q32a, k2q32b
  Values:       1 = currently has; 0 = does not currently have or never told

adhd
  Description:  Current diagnosed ADD/ADHD
  NSCH items:   k2q31a, k2q31b
  Values:       1 = currently has; 0 = does not currently have or never told

bx_problems
  Description:  Current diagnosed behavioral or conduct problems
  NSCH items:   k2q34a, k2q34b
  Values:       1 = currently has; 0 = does not currently have or never told

anxiety_severity     (NSCH item k2q33c)
depression_severity  (NSCH item k2q32c)
adhd_severity        (NSCH item k2q31c)
bxprobs_severity     (NSCH item k2q34c)
  Description:  Current moderate or severe diagnosed condition
                (Supp. Table 6)
  Values:       1 = currently has, rated moderate or severe (item "c" = 2 or
                3); 0 = currently has but mild, does not currently have, or
                never told (children without the condition are coded 0, not
                missing)

5. INDIVIDUAL-LEVEL COVARIATES
================================================================================

age
  Description:  Child age in years
  Type:         Continuous (3-17 in analytic sample)
  NSCH item:    sc_age_years

age_group
  Description:  Three-level child age group (descriptive)
  Values:       "3-5 years"; "6-11 years"; "12-17 years"
  Derived from: age

age_group_bin
  Description:  Child age group (effect modifier, Supp. Table 5)
  Values:       "3-11 years" (reference); "12-17 years"
  Derived from: age

sex
  Description:  Child sex
  Values:       "Male" (reference); "Female"
  NSCH item:    sc_sex (1 = Male, 2 = Female)

race_ethnicity
  Description:  Child race/ethnicity
  Values:       "American Indian or Alaska Native" (reference);
                "Asian, Native Hawaiian, or Pacific Islander";
                "Black or African American"; "Hispanic/Latino"; "White";
                "Other or mixed race"
  NSCH items:   sc_hispanic_r, sc_race_r
  Notes:        Hispanic/Latino children of any race are coded
                "Hispanic/Latino"; all other categories are non-Hispanic

race_ethnicity_bin
  Description:  Binary race/ethnicity (effect modifier, Supp. Table 5)
  Values:       "White" (non-Hispanic white; reference); "Non-white" (all
                other children)
  Derived from: race_ethnicity
  Notes:        Models among non-white children additionally adjust for
                race_ethnicity

nativity
  Description:  Household nativity
  Values:       "Child and or parent born out of US" (reference);
                "Child born in US, parent data missing";
                "Child and parent born in US"
  NSCH item:    house_gen (1-2; 4; 3, respectively)

fam_structure
  Description:  Family structure
  Values:       "Two parents, married" (reference); "Two parents, not
                married"; "Single parent"; "Another family structure"
  NSCH items:   family, family_r

adult_edu
  Description:  Highest educational attainment of adults in household
  Values:       "Less than high school" (reference); "High school (including
                vocational)"; "Some college or associate degree"; "College
                degree or higher"
  NSCH item:    higrade_tvis (1-4)

low_income
  Description:  Household income category used to define the analytic sample
  Values:       "Lower-income" (<200% of the federal poverty level);
                "Higher-income" (>=200% FPL)
  NSCH items:   fpl (earlier survey years) or fpl_mean, the mean of the six
                imputed FPL values fpl_i1-fpl_i6 (later survey years)
  Used in:      Sample restriction only

================================================================================
7. DERIVED VARIABLES CREATED IN THE ANALYSIS SCRIPT
   (not stored in TANF_master; created in Supp. Table 8 section)
================================================================================

G
  Description:  NSCH survey year of each state's first TANF benefit increase
  Values:       2017-2022 (because of 1-year lag, actually 2016-2021 benefit years); 0 = never increased

G2
  Description:  NSCH survey year of each state's second benefit increase
  Values:       Year; Inf = no second increase
  Notes:        Computed for reference; not used in estimation

D_first
  Description:  Size of each state's first benefit increase (main dose)
  Values:       0 for never-increased states

D_total
  Description:  Total benefit change between the 2016 and 2022 survey years
                (because of 1-year lag, actually 2015-2021 benefit years); long-difference dose

untreated
  Description:  Indicator for child-years untreated at the time of survey
  Values:       TRUE = never-increased state, or survey year before the
                state's first increase

y_adj
  Description:  Outcome residualized on individual- and state-level
                covariates, using coefficients estimated among untreated
                observations with state and year fixed effects (two-stage DiD
                covariate adjustment)

Y
  Description:  Weighted state-year mean of y_adj (panel outcome for contdid
                and did)

state_wt
  Description:  State's average weighted child count across years, normalized
                to mean = 1; used as a weight in the did event study only
